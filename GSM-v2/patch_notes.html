OVERHAUL 1

PATCH NOTES

Ability balancing, visual upgrades and combat changes.

ABILITY UPDATE

CURRENT BUILD
ECHO · NERFED

ECHO

  • Nerfed ability: Echo's burst now damages a maximum of 4 ships.
  • Animated ability: a blue energy ball forms above the ship, slowly grows, then releases a large blast.
  • Visual effect: the blast triggers screen shake for a heavier impact.
  • Balance: reduced its screen-clearing power because Echo is an early/cheaper ship.
BALANCE · COOLDOWN

ABILITY COOLDOWNS

  • Global cooldown increased: the standard ship ability cooldown is 10 seconds, with ship-specific cooldowns for certain abilities.
  • Balance: abilities are now spaced out more during combat so each activation has more impact.
ORCHID · REWORKED

ORCHID

  • New ship design: Orchid now has a flower mounted above its ship base.
  • Animated ability: the flower rotates while the Orchid ability is active.
  • Contact damage: enemies that touch Orchid's active shield take damage.
  • Shield duration doubled: Orchid's shield now lasts 8 seconds, up from 4 seconds.
ORCHID · MYTHICAL REWORK

ORCHID

  • New ability: Orchid now unleashes a large curved Flower Slash.
  • Heavy damage: anything touched by the slash takes heavy damage befitting a Mythical ship.
  • Traveling attack: the curved slash launches forward through the combat field.
  • Kill tracking: slash kills correctly award score and increase the kill counter.
  • Previous shield ability removed: Orchid no longer relies on the contact-damage shield.
ORCHID · MYTHICAL UPGRADE

ORCHID

  • Flower Slash damage increased: Flower Slash now deals 7.5× weapon damage, a 50% increase from the previous 5× damage.
  • Ship redesign: Orchid now has a more refined, organic Mythical silhouette with curved armor, a glowing inner flower core and a more distinctive profile.
  • Flower redesign: the mounted flower now has 8 alternating petals and a brighter animated core.
ORCHID · BALANCE

ORCHID

  • Ability cooldown reduced: Orchid's Mythical Flower Slash now has a 4.5-second cooldown.
  • Other ships: the standard cooldown remains 10 seconds unless a ship has a specific cooldown override.
HAUL · COMPLETE REWORK

HAUL

  • New ship design: Haul has been completely redesigned with a heavier, armored silhouette.
  • New ability — RAMPAGE: Haul creates multiple clones of itself that charge forward through the battlefield.
  • Collision damage: clones collide with enemy ships and deal heavy damage.
  • Multi-clone attack: three Haul clones are deployed in a forward rampage.
  • Kill tracking: clone kills correctly award score and increase the kill counter.
  • Impact: collisions trigger hit effects and screen shake.
HAUL · RAMPAGE UPGRADE

HAUL

  • Six-clone Rampage: Haul now creates 6 clones — 3 on each side of the player.
  • Forward charge: all six clones launch forward together and collide with enemy ships.
  • Heavy collision damage: each clone deals 5× weapon damage on impact.
  • Survivor slow: enemies that survive the Rampage are slowed to 38% movement speed for 3.5 seconds.
  • Enhanced impact: clone collisions trigger stronger screen shake and hit effects.
NOVA · REWORKED

NOVA

  • New ability: activating Nova opens a targeting prompt asking you to click the area where the ability should be used.
  • Black hole: the selected location spawns a purple/black black hole that pulls nearby ships inward.
  • Target cap: up to 4 nearby ships can be caught.
  • Damage: caught ships take heavy damage when the black hole collapses.
  • Self-destruct: the black hole collapses after its short duration and disappears with a screen-shake impact.
  • Old burst removed: Nova no longer uses the previous instant screen-wide burst.
HYDRO · VISUAL REWORK

HYDRO

  • New ability — WHIPLASH: Hydro now charges a fading blue aura before unleashing a fast water-like energy lash forward.
  • Uncommon balance: Whiplash deals 2.2× weapon damage, keeping its damage below the higher-rarity ship abilities.
  • Visual upgrade: Hydro's ability now has layered blue rings, a curved traveling lash and a bright impact burst.
  • Hit feedback: successful Whiplash hits trigger a light screen shake and still award score and kills normally.
HYDRO · BALANCE

HYDRO WHIPLASH

  • Extended reach: Whiplash now travels up to 520px.
  • Stronger curve: the lash now sweeps much farther sideways for a more dramatic whip motion.
  • Fast reload: Hydro ability cooldown reduced to 3.5 seconds.
ECONOMY · CRIX

MISSION REWARDS

  • Increased Crix earnings: score-based mission Crix is now calculated using score ÷ 2.5 instead of score ÷ 10.
  • Result: This means the score-based portion of mission rewards is 4× higher, while the existing 5,000 Crix mission cap remains.
UI · OVERHAUL 1

MISSION LOBBY

  • New boot loader: the lobby now uses an animated mission-control loader instead of plain loading text.
  • Overhaul 1 branding: the lobby and patch notes now clearly identify the current overhaul.
  • Patch 1.1 access: a dedicated button now opens the patch notes directly from the lobby.
ZED BLACK · ULTIMATE REWORK

ZED BLACK — VOID VORTEX

  • New ability: a black vortex now emerges from the tip of Zed Black.
  • Swallow effect: nearby enemy ships are dragged into the vortex and heavily damaged.
  • Scatter phase: after being swallowed, affected ships are violently scattered back outward.
  • Final detonation: the vortex explodes after the scatter phase and destroys all remaining enemy ships on the battlefield.
  • Visual upgrade: layered black rings, rotating void energy, impact flashes and heavy screen shake make the ability feel like a true ultimate.
